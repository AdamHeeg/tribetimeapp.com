Delete your TribeTime account

For the TribeTime app on Google Play

You can permanently delete your TribeTime account and its data at any time. Choose the option that works for you.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open TribeTime and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, send a request from the email address on your account. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, then reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

Or write to support@tribetimeapp.com with the subject "Delete my TribeTime account".

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and profile photo
  • Your membership in every tribe
  • Your announcement read status and notification preference
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

What is kept

Tribes you created stay available to their other members, so the group doesn't lose its calendar. If you want a tribe and all of its events and announcements removed too, delete the tribe first from the My Tribes tab (chiefs only), then delete your account.

Events and announcements on your phone's own calendar that you synced from TribeTime are stored by your device, not by us. Remove them from your device's calendar app if you no longer want them.

We don't keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our hosting provider's standard short-term backups, which roll over automatically.
